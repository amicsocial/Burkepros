Coworking location candidates for BurkePros

East Cobb is the stronger of the two areas: more high-income homeowners, well under half as many competitors, and a Google map pack that a 38-review profile can break into. Peachtree Corners is the runner-up.

Best area

East CobbCentered at The Avenue East Cobb, Johnson Ferry Rd and Roswell Rd

  1. Bigger market. About 34,000 households earning $150k+ within 5 miles, against 27,100 around Peachtree Corners.
  2. Least competition. 12 restoration companies list a street address here, about 2,830 $150k+ households each. Peachtree Corners has 30, about 900 each.
  3. Easier to rank on Google. One of East Cobb’s three map-pack spots for “water damage restoration” goes to a profile with 9 reviews. In Peachtree Corners the three spots belong to profiles with 242 to 1,100+ reviews. BurkePros has 38.
  4. More homeowners. 73% of households own their home, against 60%. Owners are the ones who file the insurance claims that pay for restoration.

Trade-offs: no coworking center sits inside the East Cobb circle. The best options are small suites on Johnson Ferry Rd that BurkePros would staff itself; the nearest staffed coworking center (HQ on Powers Ferry Rd) is 5.6 miles from the center.

What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on the suite door and on a building directory? No listing says.
  2. Is the office available now, and will you do month-to-month? Get the monthly total in writing.
  3. Is the office on the ground floor, a short walk from parking?
  4. Is there room to keep a few air movers and dehumidifiers, and what does it cost?
  5. Can BurkePros staff be there during the hours shown on Google, and will mail to the suite be accepted?

Google’s rule. Google accepts an office at a coworking space only if it has clear signage, receives customers during business hours, and is staffed by the business’s own staff during those hours.

How this was put together

Business. BurkePros Restoration, 50 Dutchtown Rd, Hampton, GA 30228, (770) 235-3512, burke-pros.com. Its Google profile is “Burkepros Fire and Water Damage Restoration” (fire damage restoration service, 38 reviews). It was not found in monday.com, so the business was confirmed from its website and Google profile. Hampton is about 37 to 39 miles south of both centers, off the maps.

Areas. The two areas were chosen by the client. East Cobb is centered at The Avenue East Cobb (4475 Roswell Rd) and Peachtree Corners at The Forum (5155 Peachtree Pkwy). Every point inside East Cobb’s ZIP codes (30062, 30066, 30067, 30068) and inside Peachtree Corners city limits was also scored: the best East Cobb circle (37,700, on the river at the Sandy Springs line) and the best Peachtree Corners circle (32,500, at the city’s north end near East Jones Bridge Rd) are shown on page 2.

Households. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 5-year estimates by census tract, via Census Reporter: B19001 (household income, $150,000 to $199,999 and $200,000+), B25003 (owner-occupied homes) and B25034 (year built). Each tract’s households were spread evenly over its land on a 0.05-mile grid, with lakes and rivers removed, and summed within each circle.

Competitors. Better Business Bureau listings in the Fire and Water Damage Restoration and Water Damage Restoration categories, searched from 10 nearby towns and read until results were more than 7 miles from both centers (418 listings). A company counts if its name or main category is restoration and it lists a street address; mailboxes, PO boxes, roofing-only listings and duplicates were removed. Addresses were placed with the Census geocoder.

Google search. Google searches and Google’s local results list (top 20, with page 2 checked for the water searches) for “water damage restoration” and “fire damage restoration” plus each area name, with the search location set to ZIP 30068 or 30092, checked November 25, 2026. Rankings move daily and differ by device and exact location.

Offices. Listings on LoopNet, Showcase, Cityfeet, Crexi, CommercialCafe, CommercialSearch, CoworkingCafe, LiquidSpace and operator sites, checked November 25, 2026. Ratings are green only when a listing says so. Listing photos could not be captured because the listing sites blocked automated access.

Map. Census TIGERweb tracts, roads and water. Shading is households earning $150,000 or more per square mile of land. Distances are straight-line miles.
